Backup of your collection

The app stores your collection in your phone’s browser storage. That is fast and convenient, but it can be deleted if you:

  • Manually clear Safari/Chrome's cache
  • Uninstall the app
  • Switch phones

The app tries to protect your data against automatic cache clearing — but nothing can protect against a deliberate delete.

A backup file is your insurance.

📱 How to store the file safely:
1. Tap "Download now" below
2. The file appears in your Files app under Downloads
3. Move it to iCloud Drive or another safe location
4. Repeat once you have added a lot of new cards
